Your Questions, Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What services does Stackly offer?
+

We provide 100% direct mutual funds with zero commissions, research-backed thematic Indian equity baskets, Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs), government fixed income, and bespoke Portfolio Management Services (PMS) for HNIs with complete tax-loss harvesting.

2. How are client securities and funds held?
+

Your assets are held directly in your personal Demat account with CDSL / NSDL under your own PAN. All transactions are cleared through RBI-approved clearing corporations (ICCL / NSE Clearing). Stackly never holds your funds on our balance sheet.

3. What is your fee structure?
+

Starter Direct is 100% free with zero brokerage on delivery and zero commission on direct mutual funds. Pro Wealth is ₹799/month for algorithmic rebalancing and research baskets. Family Office PMS is 0.35% p.a. on AUM with zero hidden kickbacks.

4. How do you ensure data security?
+

We utilize 256-bit military-grade encryption, ISO 27001 certified data centers, and SEBI-mandated two-factor authentication (2FA) for every financial operation.

5. How does Stackly differ from traditional mutual fund distributors?
+

Distributors sell regular mutual funds and pocket hidden 1% to 1.5% commissions every year from your money. Stackly is a SEBI Registered Investment Advisor (RIA) offering pure direct plans and acting as a strict fiduciary.
